# Project Philosophy — Intelligence Synthesis Engine v0.1

## Purpose

Hanani exists to synthesise geopolitical intelligence from multiple sources and
produce conclusions whose reasoning remains fully inspectable.

It is not a news aggregator, a sentiment dashboard, or a prediction market. It is
an evidence-synthesis and reasoning engine.

---

## Core Principle

Instead of asking:

> What happened?

Hanani asks:

> What do all available reports imply when combined?

Every report is a *witness*, not a verdict. Combined witnesses imply *factors*;
factors support *hypotheses*; hypotheses compete under equal scrutiny.

---

## Witness Categories

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Primary reporting | wire copy, official statements, primary documents |
| Structured data | order of battle, sanctions lists, economic series |
| OSINT | satellite imagery metadata, flight tracking, ship AIS |
| Historical precedent | analogous conflicts, doctrine, past signalling patterns |
| Cross-outlet corroboration | agreement across bias spectra |
| Cross-outlet conflict | contradictory claims requiring resolution |
| Propaganda signals | narrative framing, omission patterns, source affiliation |
| Expert / analytical frameworks | established military or diplomatic analysis models |

---

## Synthesis Principle

The system identifies underlying variables — troop movements, logistics,
ammunition consumption, industrial production, sanctions, diplomatic signalling,
satellite imagery, historical precedents, economic indicators, political
incentives, military doctrine — and debates:

- Which reports corroborate each other?
- Which conflict?
- Which appear propagandistic?
- Which assumptions drive the disagreement?
- Which conclusions are strongly supported?
- Which remain speculative?

---

## Relationship to Milcah

Milcah pressure-tests frameworks for coherence. Hanani applies the same
discipline to geopolitical hypotheses:

- equal burden across competing hypotheses,
- permitted uncertainty,
- forced certainty forbidden,
- popularity and institutional authority excluded from confidence scores.

---

## Valhalla (Orchestration)

**Valhalla** is the internal codename for Hanani's multi-agent orchestration
layer: scheduling ingestion, factor extraction, debate rounds, and trace emission.
It composes the family siblings; it does not replace them.
